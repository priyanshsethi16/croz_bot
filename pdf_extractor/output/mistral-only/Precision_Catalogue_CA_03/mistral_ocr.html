Using Edge Finders

  1. Hold the edge finder in a collet or chuck
  2. Traverse work table to obtain contact between the rotating edge finder and the work
  3. Contact will shift to a position concentric relative to the edge finder body
  4. With very slight table adjustment, contact will move off center with a distinct wobble. Stop adjustment at this point and slide back table slightly to bring the edge finder back to concentricity
  5. At this point, the center of the finder is exactly half the diameter of the contact from the work edge, thus allowing accurate location for other machining operations relative to the edge

Using Center Finders

  1. For locating center points and scribed lines, the pointed contact is used by putting a pencil or rule against the center point and running it concentrically
  2. The point of the center finder is brought down to the center point/ intersection of scribed lines and table adjusted so that when the tool just touches the work, the line up with the center point can be ascertained
