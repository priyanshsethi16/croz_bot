## Cordless Impact Drill Driver
13mm (1/2")

The Groz 20V Cordless Impact Drill Driver is a compact and powerful tool built for versatility and precision

### Lightweight Composite Construction

Minimizes user fatigue in continuous applications, overhead applications etc.

### BLDC Motor Technology

High efficiency, consistent power output, reduced downtime ensuring lower maintenance and longer tool life

### Advanced Battery Management System

Protects against overcharging, overheating and short circuits, ensuring longer battery life and reliable performance

### 23 Pre-set Torque Settings

Ensures precise fastening control, standardizes torque, prevents over driving screws & panel damage with clean finish and reduced material damage

### 2 Speed Design

Optimized speed and torque control, for efficient performance in heavy-duty and precision tasks

### Single Sleeve Keyless Metal Chuck

Enables quick one-hand bit changes, saving time and improving efficiency

### Lock-off Trigger

Prevents accidental start ensuring user safety
